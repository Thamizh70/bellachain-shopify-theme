# BellaChain Shopify Theme

A responsive luxury jewellery storefront inspired by the supplied BellaChain reference design.

## Features
- Shopify Online Store 2.0 Liquid sections and JSON templates
- Homepage hero, collection tiles, promotional banner and featured products
- Product details with variant selection and Shopify add-to-cart form
- Collection sorting, cart quantity updates, search and wishlist starter page
- Responsive desktop and mobile layouts with wine, blush and gold styling
- Shopify newsletter signup and customer account links

## Install
1. Download this repository as a ZIP.
2. In Shopify Admin, go to **Online Store → Themes → Add theme → Upload ZIP**.
3. Upload the theme folders at the ZIP root: assets, config, layout, sections, snippets and templates.
4. In **Customize**, select your hero image, collections and featured products.
5. Configure products, navigation, shipping, taxes, payment providers and store policies.

## Notes
- This is a Shopify Liquid theme, not an Angular or React application.
- The wishlist starter stores product handles in the visitor's browser. A production cross-device wishlist needs an app/backend or customer metafields.
- Product reviews require a compatible reviews app or review metafields.
- Checkout, payment processing, order history, refunds and shipping integrations depend on Shopify and the apps configured in your store.
- Replace demo image fallbacks with your licensed product and campaign photography before launch.
